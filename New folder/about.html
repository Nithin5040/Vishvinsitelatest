Mission & Vision

Mission

To be a premier IT solutions and infrastructure company — delivering end-to-end, sustainable technology solutions with proven products, precise execution and dependable support.

Vision

To delight customers and stakeholders by delivering best-in-class services that help them achieve their own business excellence.

Founded in 2017, Vishvin Technologies set out to become a preferred IT infrastructure and IT-enabled services partner, contributing to India's digital growth. The leadership is focused on building a sustainable, end-to-end solutions company — pairing proven products with precise delivery and dependable support for both government and private-sector clients.

CEO's Message

Building a company rooted in the new frontier technologies of the information age has long been the founding ambition for Vishvin. Each milestone reached opens the next, and that constant motivation now spans IT infrastructure services, smart metering and drone technology. With a focused, capable team, the goal is to keep scaling new levels of excellence for government and private clients alike, with the journey still in its early chapters.

CFO's Message — Ashok M, Director & CFO

Vishvin has always aimed to stand out through quality and competitive pricing that delivers real value. The finance team focuses on disciplined capital allocation and shareholder returns, guided by three growth levers: controlling the cost of capital, optimizing short-term profit, and maximizing long-term value across the organization.

Director's Message — S Venkatesh Murthy, Director

Steering marketing and projects at a pivotal moment of transformation, the focus is on embracing new frontier technologies while keeping quality products and customer delight at the center. In just a few years Vishvin became a recognized name in ITeS and IT infrastructure for utilities, with ambitions to lead in geospatial drone applications and IoT-based smart metering.
